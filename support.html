Route Rush Support

Need help with Route Rush Wooster? Send support requests to lukem2183@gmail.com.

What To Include

  • Your Route Rush nickname.
  • The email address on your account.
  • The screen or feature where you saw the issue.
  • A short description of what happened.

Location And Route Issues

Route Rush uses your device location only when you choose to check in or start a route. If check-ins or route tracking are not working, confirm that location permissions are enabled for Route Rush in your device settings.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from the Profile screen. If you need help with account deletion, email support from the address on your Route Rush account.
